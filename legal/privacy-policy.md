2. The legal grounds of processing your personal data

   The legal grounds that Yobbi relies upon while processing your personal data under the GDPR and applicable German data protection law are the following:

   In data processing activities where your personal data is processed based on explicit consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. The withdrawal of consent shall not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

   Yobbi's legitimate interest in processing your device and usage data is to provide better service to you, to make suggestions, to improve its services, to improve and update our Games, and to facilitate the use of Games in relation to your interests and preferences, within the framework of legal legislation.

   Yobbi undertakes to keep your data and any confidential information strictly private and confidential, to regard it as an obligation to keep confidentiality, to take all appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent all or any part of your information from entering into the public domain or being subject to unauthorized use. If, despite all necessary data security measures taken by Yobbi, personal data is compromised as a result of attacks on the system or captured by third parties, Yobbi shall notify the relevant supervisory authority and, where applicable, the affected users without undue delay in accordance with Art. 33 and Art. 34 GDPR.

4. Your Rights Regarding the Protection of Your Personal Data

	Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

   * **Right of access** (Art. 15 GDPR): the right to obtain confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning you is being processed, and to access such data.
   * **Right to rectification** (Art. 16 GDPR): the right to obtain rectification of inaccurate personal data.
   * **Right to erasure** (Art. 17 GDPR): the right to obtain the erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") under certain conditions.
   * **Right to restriction of processing** (Art. 18 GDPR): the right to obtain restriction of processing under certain conditions.
   * **Right to data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR): the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller.
   * **Right to object** (Art. 21 GDPR): the right to object to processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests, including profiling.
   * **Right to withdraw consent** (Art. 7(3) GDPR): where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time.
   * **Right to lodge a complaint** (Art. 77 GDPR): the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent supervisory authority is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Hamburgischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

	You can submit all your claims and questions with respect to such rights to info@yobbigames.com or via https://www.yobbigames.com/#contact. Your requests will be answered without undue delay and within one month at the latest, in accordance with Art. 12(3) GDPR.
