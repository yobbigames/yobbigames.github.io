A troupe of toy animals goes on tour with no money, odd talents and a producer who believes in them a little too much. Between shows you hire acts, buy Props and merge three of a kind into a star; on stage they fight on their own, and your job is to line up the Ultimates so a SHOWSTOPPER lands when it matters.

Runs are short, the daily and weekly shows share a seed with everyone else, and in Twitch mode your chat sits in the crowd and votes on what to throw.
