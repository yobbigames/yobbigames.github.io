Every book comes back, and it comes back on the belt. Someone shelves by colour, someone works the desk, and two of you take the Binding Press, because it will not close for one. Each wing of the library has its own rule, and five Big Days (the Book Fair, the Inspector, the Author Signing, the Power Cut, Story Hour) break whatever routine you had.

Plays on one screen, online through Steam, or over Remote Play Together. On your own, the Bookworm, the Owl and the cat help out.
