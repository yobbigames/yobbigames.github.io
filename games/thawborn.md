Thawborn is a survival strategy game for friends: 1v1, 2v2 or four tribes in a free-for-all, each led by a hero who walks the map with their villagers. One winter escalates over a single match. Keep your fire warm, raid your rival's stores, drive the herds away from them, or let their starving villagers walk over to you.

It is early. We will show it when there is something to play.
